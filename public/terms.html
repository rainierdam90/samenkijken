1. What SameCouch is

SameCouch lets you open a private room, watch videos in sync, see and hear each other, chat, share photos and videos from your device, and keep a shared memories wall. Up to 8 people can be in a room. SameCouch is free: there are no accounts, no subscriptions and no payments.

We don't check your subscription, so this is your responsibility.

If you think we made a mistake, e-mail us and we will look at it again.

6. What the room host can do

The person who creates a room is its host. The host can set a password, remove items from the memories wall and remove participants. Removing someone is temporary — it is not a permanent ban — so if you want to keep people out, set a password and only share it with the people you invite. People who posted an item on the wall can also remove it themselves from the same device.

7. Your privacy

How we handle your data is explained in our Privacy Policy. In short: calls are encrypted and go directly between devices, chat is kept for at most 24 hours, and the memories wall is deleted 12 months after the room was last used.
