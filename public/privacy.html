Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

SameCouch is a free website where you can watch videos together, see and hear each other, chat and keep a shared memories wall. This page explains in plain language what information is involved, why, who else is involved, how long things are kept and what your rights are. It follows the European privacy law (the GDPR, called AVG in the Netherlands).

The short version

  • No account, no adverts, no tracking cookies — and we never sell your data.
  • Your video and voice call goes directly between the people in your room and is encrypted. We cannot watch, listen to or record it.
  • Photos and videos you share in the room are kept on our server for at most 6 hours, only for the people in your room.
  • Chat messages pass through our server, are only kept in its memory and are deleted at the latest 24 hours after the room was last used.
  • What you put on the memories wall is stored so your room can see it again later. It is deleted automatically 12 months after the room was last used, or sooner if you ask.
  • Questions or requests? E-mail rainier@nexthorizonsglobal.com.

Who is responsible

SameCouch is responsible for the personal data processed through samecouch.com (the "controller" under the GDPR). For any privacy question, to use your rights, or to report something, e-mail rainier@nexthorizonsglobal.com.

What we use, why, and for how long

Your name in the room

When you join a room you type a name. It can be anything — a nickname is fine. It is shown to the people in your room, appears next to your chat messages, and is saved with anything you post on the memories wall. Your browser also remembers it on your own device, so you don't have to type it again.

Video and voice calls

Your camera and microphone only work after you allow them in your browser. The call goes directly from device to device (peer-to-peer) and is encrypted (WebRTC with DTLS-SRTP). If a direct connection isn't possible, the encrypted call is passed on through our relay server (a "TURN" server), which cannot read it. We never record calls. Up to 8 people can be in a room.

Photos and videos you share in the room

When you use "Share photos/videos" (from your computer, or by scanning the QR code with your phone), each file is uploaded once to our server in the United Kingdom over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) and streamed from there to the people in your room. The files can only be reached with your room's private token and are never public. They are deleted automatically after at most 6 hours, and 1 hour after the room becomes empty. The person who shared them can also stop sharing and remove them sooner. If our server can't be used, the app sends the file directly from device to device instead, encrypted (WebRTC/DTLS).

Chat messages

Chat messages are sent through our server so everyone in the room receives them. The server keeps the last 300 messages per room in its memory, so people who join later can read what was said. They are deleted automatically 24 hours after the room was last active, and also every time the server restarts. Chat messages are never written to our database. For safety, the operator can read a room's recent chat when investigating abuse — that's why the chat says "Messages may be reviewed for safety". Chat is not end-to-end encrypted.

Memories wall

Notes, photos (up to about 3 MB), voice messages and short videos you post on the wall are stored in our database, together with your name and the time, so your room can see them again later. They are deleted automatically 12 months after the room was last used. The room host can remove items, and the person who posted an item can remove it from the same device. Anyone can report an item with the report button; we then look at that item and the room it belongs to, and we remove illegal or abusive content. You can ask us to delete a room or its wall at any time by e-mailing us the room link.

Room settings

For each room we store the room code, the chosen theme and decorations, and — if the host sets one — the room password. The password is only stored as a salted scrypt hash, a scrambled form that cannot be turned back into the password. Room settings are deleted together with the wall, 12 months after the room was last used.

Videos you watch together

  • YouTube and Vimeo. Only when you or someone in your room plays one of their videos, it is loaded from YouTube (using the privacy-enhanced youtube-nocookie.com player) or Vimeo. They then receive your IP address and other technical information, and their own privacy policies apply.
  • Discover (Internet Archive, radio, PeerTube). When you search in “Discover”, your search words go through our server to the Internet Archive, the radio-browser.info directory or the PeerTube search index (SepiaSearch). They don’t see who you are, and we only keep results briefly in server memory to make it faster. When someone in your room plays something, each viewer’s browser loads it directly from that source — archive.org, the radio station’s own stream server, or the PeerTube server that hosts the video — which then receives your IP address and other technical information. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Video links and subtitles. If you paste a link to a video file, our server may briefly check the link and, if your browser can't play the file directly, convert it on the fly so it can. The video then passes through our server but is not saved. Subtitle files you choose are shared with your room and kept only in the server's memory while the room is in use. The shared watchlist, votes, reactions and saved moments are also only kept in memory while the room is in use.

IPTV (optional)

You can connect your own IPTV provider. Before connecting, you must confirm that you have a legal subscription and that your provider allows you to watch together with the people in your room. Your provider's server address, username and password (or playlist link) are then sent to our server. They are kept only in the server's memory for that session — at most 12 hours, and removed earlier when you disconnect or the room empties. They are never stored in our database or logs and never shared with the other people in your room (they only get an anonymous token). The video stream from your provider passes through our server, which may convert it so browsers can play it, and is sent on to the people in your room. We do not save programmes.

Push notifications (optional)

If you ask to be notified when someone is in a room, or set a watch-party reminder, we store your browser's push subscription (a technical address for your browser) and your name. This is kept until you turn notifications off, your browser cancels the subscription, or the room is deleted. A reminder is deleted once it has been sent. The notification itself is delivered by your browser maker's push service (for example Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft).

Visitor statistics

To count how many different visitors we have each day, the server stores a scrambled code made from your IP address (a keyed HMAC hash) that changes every day. We never store your actual IP address for this. These codes are deleted after 120 days. We don't use analytics services or tracking cookies.

Technical logs, security and backups

  • Our server's web access logs (IP address, time and the address that was requested) are kept for 14 days, to keep the service safe and to find faults. The server also briefly uses IP addresses in its memory to limit misuse, such as too many requests at once.
  • Our website host Vercel may also record IP addresses in its access logs.
  • Every night we make an encrypted backup of our database, which is kept for 14 days. Something you deleted can therefore remain in a backup for up to 14 days before it disappears for good. Chat messages and IPTV logins are never in backups, because they are never in the database.
  • All connections use encryption (HTTPS/TLS), and access to the servers is limited to the operator.

Saved on your own device

SameCouch uses your browser's local storage — not tracking cookies — to remember: your name, your language, the rooms you visited, the size of the camera tiles, and the keys that let you remove your own wall posts. Only if you tick "Remember login" are your IPTV server, username and password also saved there, in plain text, on that device. Untick it, or clear your browser's site data, to remove them. Do not use "Remember login" on a shared computer. Clips made with "Save this moment" are recorded only on your own device and stay there unless you choose to save or share them yourself; please ask the people in a clip before sharing it. This information stays on your device and is only used to make the features you choose work; you can delete it at any time through your browser settings.

Connection check (optional)

If you start a connection check, your browser exchanges small test messages with our server and tests the relay server. The test data is thrown away immediately and nothing is recorded.

Why we are allowed to use this information

  • To provide the service you ask for (GDPR article 6(1)(b)): rooms, calls, chat, the memories wall, sharing and IPTV.
  • Our legitimate interests (article 6(1)(f)): keeping the service secure, preventing and handling abuse, access logs, counting visitors and making backups. We keep this to the minimum, and you may object (see your rights below).
  • Your consent (article 6(1)(a)): push notifications, and the use of your camera and microphone, which you allow through your browser. You can withdraw your consent at any time — this doesn't affect what happened before.

Who else is involved

  • Vercel (USA) hosts the website itself and may log IP addresses. Transfers to the USA are protected by the safeguards in Vercel's data processing terms, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Netwise Hosting Ltd (London, United Kingdom) provides the virtual server that runs our room server, chat relay, IPTV gateway and relay (TURN) server, and that temporarily holds the photos and videos you share in a room. The European Commission has decided that the UK protects personal data adequately (an "adequacy decision").
  • netcup GmbH (Germany) stores our encrypted nightly database backups.
  • Google: when your browser sets up a call, it contacts Google's STUN servers (stun.l.google.com), which see your IP address. The app's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which also sees your IP address.
  • YouTube and Vimeo, only when you or someone in your room plays their videos. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Internet Archive, radio stations and PeerTube servers, only when you or someone in your room plays something from “Discover”. Their own privacy policies apply.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft), only if you turn on notifications.
  • Your own IPTV provider, only when you use IPTV.
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it, or to report serious crimes such as child sexual abuse material.

We don't show adverts, don't use analytics or tracking cookies, don't build profiles and never sell your data. We don't make automated decisions about you.

Your rights

You have the right to:

  • see the personal data we hold about you (access);
  • have it corrected (rectification) or deleted (erasure);
  • ask us to temporarily stop using it (restriction);
  • object to us using it on the basis of our legitimate interests;
  • receive data you gave us in a common format, or have it sent to someone else (data portability);
  • withdraw your consent at any time, for example by turning off notifications or camera access in your browser.

E-mail rainier@nexthorizonsglobal.com. Because SameCouch has no accounts, please include the room link so we can find your data; we may ask a question to check that the request really comes from you. We reply within one month. If you are not happy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) at autoriteitpersoonsgegevens.nl, or to the data protection authority in your own country.

Do you have to give us information?

No. You don't need an account, e-mail address or real name. Without camera or microphone permission you can still watch and chat; some features, like notifications or IPTV, only work if you choose to use them.

Minimum age

You must be at least 16 to use SameCouch, or have permission from a parent or guardian if you are younger. Rooms are private: they can only be reached with the link, and there is no public listing or random matching.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle data, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes we will also put a notice on the website.
